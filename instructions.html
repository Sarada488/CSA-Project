Security Simulator Instructions

You are a Security Operations Center analyst. Your job is to detect, investigate, and respond to cyber attacks.

How to Play

  • Alerts will appear automatically in the dashboard
  • Each alert represents a possible security incident
  • You must choose actions like Investigate or Isolate Host
  • Correct actions increase XP and reduce threat score
  • Wrong actions increase risk and threat level

Commands

  • status → shows your current stats
  • scan [host] → investigate a machine
  • logs [host] → view activity logs
  • help → list commands

Goal

Complete scenarios by containing cyber attacks before they spread.
